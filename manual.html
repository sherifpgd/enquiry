Admission Enquiry Tracker

Record every admission enquiry in one place, contact each family the same or next day, and follow them to a school visit and admission. This manual shows every role how, step by step.

At a glance

Staff and partners use the tracker; parents use the enquiry form. All data lives in the school's Google Sheet and is shared live between everyone who signs in.

The admission workflow

Getting started

You need three things from your administrator: your user name, a temporary password and the web app address (it starts with https://script.google.com/macros/s/). Do this once on each computer or phone:

  1. Open sherifpgd.github.io/enquiry in Chrome, Edge or Safari.
  2. Go to Settings → Shared database & sign-in, paste the web app address and tap Connect.
  3. Sign in with your user name and temporary password.
  4. Go to Settings (My account) → Change password and choose your own (8+ characters).
  5. On a computer, tap Turn on reminders on the Dashboard for due follow-ups and new online enquiries.

Install on your phone so it opens like an app: Android (Chrome) ⋮ → Add to Home screen; iPhone (Safari) Share → Add to Home Screen.

Light or dark: tap one of the three theme icons at the bottom of the menu – screen (Auto, follows your device), sun (Light) or moon (Dark). On a phone they are under More → Theme. The choice is saved on that device.

On shared office computers, always sign out (Settings or More → Sign out). Signing out removes the enquiry data from that computer. Five wrong passwords lock your user name for 15 minutes.

Your daily routine (counsellors)

Every working day, clear your Follow-ups list from the top down and log every contact.

Morning (about 30 minutes)

  1. Read the 7 AM email “Your admission follow-ups” (or open Dashboard).
  2. Open Visit calendar → Visits today: check who is coming and when.
  3. Open Follow-ups → Overdue: call or WhatsApp each family, then tap Log follow-up.
  4. Then Follow-ups → Due today, the same way.

During the day

  1. Contact every new enquiry the same day (stage “New” under Follow-ups → Due today, plus a notification if reminders are on).
  2. After each school visit, tap ✓ Visited (or No-show) on the visit calendar.
  3. Record anything a parent tells you (fees, transport, siblings, curriculum) in the follow-up note.

Before you leave

  1. Open Visit calendar → Visits tomorrow and tap Remind all.
  2. Check Overdue is empty. If not, call those families first tomorrow.
Three rules keep the system useful: every enquiry has a next follow-up date, every contact is logged, and every closed lead has a reason.

  • Phone / WhatsApp is the only required field; type it any way (050…, 50…, +971…).
  • If the number already exists, a yellow box says so: tap Open existing rather than creating a duplicate.
  • Add each child with their class (+ Add child for siblings).

  • A job seeker? The form warns you: tap Mark as job enquiry.

What Paste understands: WhatsApp messages and chat exports (it takes the sender's number and the real date and time), the online form, Facebook/Instagram lead ads, emails, and free text. It recognises classes written many ways (KG1, LKG, FS2, Year 3, Grade 3, 3rd grade, “الصف 3”), suggests a class from a child's age (“my son is 4” → KG1), splits “two kids, KG1 and G2” into two children, and reads Arabic digits and common Arabic and Malayalam words. Fields it filled are highlighted in green – check them before saving. After saving, it offers to send the First reply on WhatsApp straight away.

Links and QR codes (Admin): Settings → Online enquiry form & QR codes, choose a source and campaign, then Copy link or Download QR. Use a different link or QR for every poster, banner and ad.

Following up

Call or message the family, then log it straight away: the tracker then sets the next step for you.

  • Call: tap Call. On a phone it dials; the Log follow-up form opens for when you hang up.
  • WhatsApp: tap WhatsApp, choose a ready-made message, tap Open WhatsApp, press Send in WhatsApp, come back and save the follow-up.
  • Log: choose the Outcome, write what was said in one line, check the suggested stage and date, tap Save follow-up.
OutcomeWhat the tracker does
AnsweredStage → Contacted (if New); next follow-up in 3 days
No answer / Busy / Switched offStage → No Response; next follow-up tomorrow; after 3 in a row it suggests Not Reachable
Message sentNext follow-up in 2 days
Visited campusStage → Visited; next follow-up in 2 days
Wrong numberStage → Invalid; no further follow-up

Lead scores – Hot, Warm, Cold – show who to call first. Sort All enquiries by Lead score.

Ready-made WhatsApp messages: First reply, No response, Visit confirmation, Visit reminder, Thank you after visit, Documents pending, Next year, Job reply (Admin edits them in Settings).

School visits

  1. On a family's card tap Book visit (or + Book in a free calendar slot).
  2. Pick date and time (full slots are marked).
  3. Keep Send WhatsApp confirmation ticked → Book visit → Open WhatsApp.

The stage becomes Visit Scheduled and the next follow-up is the day before. The day before: Visits tomorrow → Remind all. On the day: ✓ Visited or No-show (back to Interested, rebook today). After: send Thank you after visit and agree the next step.

Closed leads leave Follow-ups but stay in All enquiries and Reports. If a closed family enquires again, it reopens as New. Job enquiries go to the Job enquiries page: tap HR reply, and it closes.

Reception's tasks

  1. Every call, walk-in and message: + New enquiry or Paste enquiry; watch for the yellow “already exists” box.
  2. Choose Assigned to (or leave unassigned for counsellors to pick up).
  3. Book visits for walk-ins and callers; send the confirmation.
  4. When a family arrives, tap ✓ Visited under Visits today.
  5. Job seekers: save as a job enquiry and send the HR reply.

Partners (view only)

Partners of the school can see everything but change nothing. Pages show a View only tag and the add, edit, delete, call and WhatsApp buttons are hidden.

  • Find enquiries: All enquiries → search by name, phone, area or notes, and filter by status, type, stage, source, counsellor or lead score.
  • See the details: tap any family to see children, classes, stage, visit, documents, fees and the full history of calls and messages.
  • Reports: every Reports tab and period; click underlined numbers to see the families behind them.
  • Visits and seats: the Visit calendar and Seats pages.
  • Your password: Settings (My account) → Change password.

The school can also email partners a daily or weekly report with the sections they want (see Report emails).

Report emails

The Google Sheet emails reports automatically to any address the Admin adds – staff, the principal or partners. Each address chooses how often and which reports.

  1. Admin: Settings → Report emails → + Add email.
  2. Type the address and choose Daily (7:30 AM), Weekly (Monday 8 AM) or Daily and weekly.
  3. Keep All reports ticked, or untick it and pick the sections this person needs.
  4. Tap Send test to check the email arrives.

Problems and questions

ProblemFix
Offline · N unsentNo internet. Keep working; changes upload when you're back online. Don't sign out until it says Synced.
“Your session has ended”Sign in again (after 30 days, a password change, or if your account was disabled).
“Too many wrong passwords”Wait 15 minutes or ask the Admin to reset it.
I can't see a familyCounsellors see only their own and unassigned leads; ask a Manager to reassign it.
“Not allowed for your role and undone”Your role can't make that change. Ask a Manager or the Admin.
Buttons are missing / “View only”You have the Partner role: you can look but not change anything.
WhatsApp opens but nothing is sentPress Send in WhatsApp.
A report email didn't arriveCheck spam; ask the Admin to tap Send test for your address.
The page looks old after an updateClose and reopen the tracker.

Who to ask: logins, settings and report emails – the school's tracker Admin. Assignments and reports – your Manager.

Privacy: the tracker holds parents' names and phone numbers. Don't share screenshots or exports outside the school, and always sign out on shared computers.
